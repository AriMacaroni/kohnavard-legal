Kohnavard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data Kohnavard collects, why, and what rights you have under the GDPR. Controller: developer of Kohnavard. Contact: kohnavard.app@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: email and password (hashed by our auth provider).
  • Profile: name, date of birth (18+ gate only, not public), bio, optional contact links, avatar, home city.
  • Activity: adventures, RSVPs, posts, stories, likes, comments, follows, blocks, reports, messages, photos.
  • Location (optional): approximate and/or precise, only with permission, to show nearby activity. You can search a city manually instead of GPS.
  • Technical: language preference; push token if notifications are enabled.

2. Purposes

We use data to run the service, keep it safe, send optional notifications, and personalise Explore. We do not sell data or use it for third-party advertising.

3. Sharing

Public profile fields and public posts are visible to other signed-in users. Email, date of birth, and private messages are not public. Processors include Supabase, Google Places (via our server), Expo/Apple/Google for push and distribution, and Resend for operator safety alerts.

4. Your rights and deletion

In the app: Settings → Delete account.

Without the app: see Account deletion or email kohnavard.app@gmail.com with subject Account deletion.

You may access, correct, restrict, object, port data, and complain to a supervisory authority (in Italy: the Garante).

5. UGC safety

You can report content/profiles; we aim to review within 48 hours. You can block other users. We may remove content or accounts that breach the Terms.

6. Children

Kohnavard is not intended for anyone under 18.

This page is not legal advice. Have a GDPR-qualified lawyer review it before a public multi-country launch.
