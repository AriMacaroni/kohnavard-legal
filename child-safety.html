Viamici — Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 27 September 2026 · App: Viamici (io.kohnavard.app)

Viamici is a social outdoor-adventures app for adults. These published standards explain how we prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and how we handle related reports. They apply to the Viamici mobile app and related services operated under the Viamici name.

1. Age requirement

Viamici is strictly for users aged 18 and over. We use an age gate at registration. We do not knowingly allow accounts for minors. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we will remove it.

2. Zero tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

Viamici prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including any child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualisation of minors, or attempts to involve minors in sexual content or contact. Such content and behaviour are banned on Viamici without exception.

3. Reporting and in-app feedback

Users can report posts, comments, messages, stories, and profiles from inside the app. Users can also block other users. We aim to review reports within 48 hours and may remove content or accounts that breach these standards or our Terms.

4. Action on CSAM

When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM or credible CSAE on Viamici, we take appropriate action, including removing the content, restricting or terminating accounts, preserving evidence where required by law, and reporting confirmed CSAM to the relevant authority (for example NCMEC in the United States, or the competent authority in the applicable country).

5. Compliance with child safety laws

We maintain processes intended to comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations relevant to the operation of Viamici, including reporting obligations for confirmed CSAM where they apply.

6. Child safety point of contact

For CSAE / child-safety notifications related to Viamici (including from Google Play), contact: owl@viamici.com (or info@viamici.com). This contact can discuss enforcement and take action when required.
